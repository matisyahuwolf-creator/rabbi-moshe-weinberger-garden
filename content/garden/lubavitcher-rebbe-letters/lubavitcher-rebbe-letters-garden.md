# Lubavitcher Rebbe Letters Garden

## Purpose

This garden is for researching the Lubavitcher Rebbe's letters as Torah guidance: how the Rebbe answers people, frames problems, gives horaos, strengthens bitachon, and translates Chassidus into lived avodah.

The letters should become a source corpus with topic pages, recurring language, cross-links, and curated paths.

## Letter Pattern

- Date and recipient context, when available.
- Main question or life situation.
- The Rebbe's core guidance.
- Sources or quoted ideas.
- Practical horaah.
- Connected concepts.
- Similar letters.

- What kinds of situations repeat across the letters?
- Which phrases or horaos return again and again?
- How does the Rebbe connect practical advice to emunah, bitachon, and avodah?
- Which letters belong together as a learning path?
