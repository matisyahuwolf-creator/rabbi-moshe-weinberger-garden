# Sefer HaArachim Garden

## Purpose

This garden is for building a concept-by-concept map of Chabad Chassidus through Sefer HaArachim.

The goal is not only to collect entries. The goal is to turn each entry into a durable research node: definition, source trail, inner structure, related concepts, and practical avodah.

## Entry Pattern

- Name of the erech.
- Short working definition.
- Primary sources.
- Related entries.
- Key contrasts and distinctions.
- Where this appears in maamarim, sichos, and shiurim.
- Avodah takeaway.

## Research Questions

- What does each term mean in its own language?
- Which sources are the roots of the entry?
- Which related entries need to be learned together?
- How does the concept change when it moves from haskalah into avodah?
