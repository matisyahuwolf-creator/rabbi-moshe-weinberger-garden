# Tanya

## Purpose

This note is for Tanya as the map of the inner life: nefesh habehamis, nefesh haElokis, beinoni, thought/speech/action, joy, struggle, and avodah.

## Core Ideas

Tanya gives language for the reality that a person can have huge ideals and messy inner resistance at the same time. It makes struggle normal without making weakness the identity.

## Personal Relevance

A lot of my work is about turning inspiration into action. Tanya helps frame that: the goal is not to wait until I feel perfect. The goal is to act like a beinoni — disciplined action even when the inner world is noisy.
