# Torah and Chassidus Graph

## Purpose

This is the map for building a connected Torah and Chassidus knowledge base.

The goal is not just to collect vortlach. The goal is to see how concepts connect: Tanya, maamarim, parsha, Gemara, halacha, avodah, psychology, and daily life.

## Core Vision

A living Torah graph where concepts like **ohr**, **keli**, **bittul**, **hisbonenus**, **nefesh habehamis**, **nefesh haElokis**, **bitachon**, and **dirah b’tachtonim** are connected to examples, sources, personal avodah, and practical life.
