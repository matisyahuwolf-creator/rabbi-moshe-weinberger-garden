# Research Map

This garden begins with the question Rabbi Weinberger returns to in the opening shiurim: what is the chiddush of [[Chassidus|Chassidus]] after the appearance of the [[Baal Shem Tov|Baal Shem Tov]]?

## Threads

- [[Mahapecha|Mahapecha]] as the frame for the Baal Shem Tov's arrival.
- [[Tzimtzum|Tzimtzum]] as a core doorway into how Chassidus reads creation, concealment, and presence.
- [[Keser Shem Tov|Keser Shem Tov]] as the textual center of many later classes.
- [[Izhbitz|Izhbitz]] and [[Kotzk|Kotzk]] as a biography-centered way to see competing forms of avodah.

## Working questions

- Where does Rabbi Weinberger distinguish Chassidus from Mussar, Kabbalah, and philosophy?
- Which terms are being misheard by the transcription model and need a glossary pass?
- Which shiurim should become polished essay-length fruits?
