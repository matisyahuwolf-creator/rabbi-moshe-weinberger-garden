# Heichal HaChassidus

This is the built structure made from the garden's pieces: themes, concepts, sugya tracks, and source shiurim. The network map shows all the roads; this page builds those roads into a place you can walk through.

## Rooms

### The Letter Room

The entrance is the Baal Shem Tov's letter: [[concepts/Baal Shem Tov Letter|Baal Shem Tov Letter]], [[concepts/Chiddush HaBaal Shem Tov|Chiddush HaBaal Shem Tov]], and [[concepts/Moshiach|Moshiach]]. This room asks what changed in the world when the wellsprings began moving outward.

### The Light Engine

Here [[concepts/Kabbalah|Kabbalah]], [[concepts/Arizal|Arizal]], [[concepts/Pnimius HaTorah|Pnimius HaTorah]], and [[concepts/Or|Or]] become more than vocabulary. They become a way to see why the world needed a new light.

### The Oneness Chamber

This is the deepest load-bearing room: [[concepts/Ein Od Milvado|Ein Od Milvado]], [[concepts/Tzimtzum|Tzimtzum]], [[concepts/Hashem|Hashem]], and [[concepts/Shechinah|Shechinah]]. Everything else in the building depends on whether this room is alive.

### The Workshop of Ordinary Life

This room is where Chassidus becomes practical: [[concepts/Avodah Begashmius|Avodah Begashmius]], [[concepts/Heart|Heart]], [[concepts/Hashgacha Pratis|Hashgacha Pratis]], and [[concepts/Deveikus|Deveikus]]. Food, darkness, travel, and Torah are not side rooms; they are the workbench.

### The Soul Studio

Here the person being built is the building material: [[concepts/Neshama|Neshama]], [[concepts/Ratzon|Ratzon]], [[concepts/Emunah|Emunah]], [[concepts/Yirah|Yirah]], and [[concepts/Simcha|Simcha]].

### The Keser Library

The upper floor gathers the long textual cycle: [[themes/keser-shem-tov-textual-cycle|Keser Shem Tov Textual Cycle]]. After the structure is built, this is where you keep returning to learn how each brick speaks inside the Baal Shem Tov's Torah.
